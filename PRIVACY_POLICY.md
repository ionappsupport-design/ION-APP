## Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains what information the **ION Cleaner** app ("ION", "the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the ION Android app and to ionapp.support-related web services. This policy is written to match the Data Safety declaration submitted to Google Play.

---

## 1. Our Core Data Principle: Your Files Stay on Your Device
**ION is built as an offline-first app.**  
When ION scans your photos, videos, documents, or audio files to find junk, duplicates, or large files, **that scanning happens entirely on your device locally**.  
* The contents of your files are **never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers**.
* We cannot view, access, or share your media or personal files.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We only collect limited non-file information necessary to provide authentication and improve app stability:
- **Account Information:** Name and email address (if you create an account via Firebase Auth or Google Sign-In).
- **App Usage & Diagnostic Data:** App version, device model, and crash logs to fix bugs and improve performance.
- **Support Inquiries:** Information you voluntarily submit through forms, feedback, or support requests.

We only request permissions that are strictly necessary for core functionality:
- **Storage Access (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Media Permissions):** Required locally on-device to scan junk cache, duplicate media, and free up storage space.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the collected information solely to:
- Authenticate users and manage your account or VIP Pro entitlement.
- Maintain app security and prevent fraud or misuse.
- Monitor stability, diagnose crashes, and fix performance issues.
- Respond to your support inquiries.

---

## 4. Information Sharing
**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information.**  
We only share information with trusted infrastructure service providers strictly required to operate the App:
- **Google Firebase:** For secure user authentication and crash reporting.
- **Razorpay:** For secure payment gateway processing when upgrading to VIP Pro. (Payment credentials are processed directly by Razorpay under 256-bit encryption; ION never stores card or UPI details).

---

## 5. Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
